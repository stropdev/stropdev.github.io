What changed

The last 3 releases, newest first. Tarballs and checksums are on GitHub releases.

0.68.0 — 2026-11-14

Changed

  • A new default theme, kiln: warm charcoal with a terracotta accent and cream text, after the Claude Code terminal look. It meets the same contrast minimums as every other palette. hone stays available with :theme hone or theme = "hone".

0.67.0 — 2026-11-13

Changed

  • Splits nest, as in Vim (plans/0104). :sp then :vs divides the pane you are in and leaves the others where they were; before, the last split turned every pane the same way. The new pane opens next to the one you split, below or to the right, and panes are numbered in reading order.
  • Closing a pane gives its space to its neighbour, and the focus goes there, as in Vim.
  • Ctrl-W h/j/k/l and Space w h/j/k/l go to the pane on that side, at the cursor's line or column, and stop at the edge instead of wrapping around.
  • Dividing lines join the title lines where they meet: ┬ ├ ┤ ┼ ┴.
  • How a split's space is shared is proved. The arithmetic lives in the kernel and Lean proofs show the panes never overlap or run into the status line, fill their space exactly when there is room, each get at least a line, and are shared fairly by the number of panes across.

Fixed

  • A broken link in the git guide to the lock files section.

0.66.0 — 2026-11-13

Changed

  • Each file in a collection starts at a thick line (plans/0103). Search results, collected hits and the quickfix list are one buffer of many files; where one file's lines end and the next file's begin is now drawn with a heavy rule (━━━━ src/b.rs ━━━━ 2 hits), bright in every file, not only the one the cursor is in. Status sections, the diff's file headings and the picker keep the thin rule.
  • Split panes lose their corners. A pane title is a thin rule (─ 1 src/main.rs ● ───), brighter on the focused pane. Side by side, one vertical line runs the full height and meets the titles in a ┬.
  • Stacked panes get a row back. The closing line under the upper pane is gone; the lower pane's title line is the boundary.

Fixed

  • Quitting right after starting no longer crashes. If :q came before strop finished reading an earlier session's unsaved drafts, the editor panicked on its way out. The drafts are kept for next time.
